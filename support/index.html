技术支持

Support

本地素材快捷浏览支持 macOS 14.0 或更高版本，以及 Apple 芯片和 Intel 处理器。

Local Media Quick Browser requires macOS 14.0 or later and supports both Apple silicon and Intel processors.

快速开始 · Quick Start

  1. 打开应用。点击“选择文件夹”，或把文件夹拖到应用窗口。Open the app and choose a folder, or drag a folder onto the window.
  2. 等待扫描。媒体会在扫描过程中分批显示，无需等待全部完成。Media appears in batches while scanning; you do not need to wait for completion.
  3. 浏览目录。在侧边栏选择文件夹，右侧会显示该文件夹及所有后代目录中的媒体。Select a folder in the sidebar to show media in that folder and all descendants.
  4. 选择与预览。单击选择，按住 Command 切换多选，或按住 Shift 范围选择。按空格快速查看，用四个方向键导航。Click to select, Command-click to toggle multiple items, or Shift-click to select a range. Press Space for Quick Look and navigate with all four arrow keys.

常用功能 · Common Features

搜索与筛选

按文件名或相对路径搜索，并在全部、图片和视频之间筛选。

Search by filename or relative path and filter by all, images, or videos.

排序与缩略图

按名称、修改日期或文件大小排序，并调整缩略图尺寸。

Sort by name, modification date, or file size, and adjust thumbnail size.

多选与键盘导航

支持 Finder 风格多选、Command-A 全选当前结果，以及四方向键网格导航。

Use Finder-style multi-selection, Command-A for current results, and four-direction keyboard navigation.

Finder 与路径

复制文件或文件夹路径，也可以直接在 Finder 中显示。

Copy file or folder paths, or reveal items directly in Finder.

Pro 永久版 · Pro Lifetime

Pro 永久版是一次性买断的非消耗型内购，无试用，支持家人共享。中国大陆定价为 ¥18，美国定价为 $8.99，其他地区价格以 App Store 显示为准。

Pro Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with no trial and Family Sharing enabled. It is priced at CNY 18 in mainland China and USD 8.99 in the United States; the App Store displays localized prices elsewhere.

重复与相似素材

完全重复识别覆盖图片和视频；视觉相似识别覆盖图片。文件哈希和视觉特征均仅在 Mac 本地计算。

Exact duplicate detection covers photos and videos; visual similarity detection covers photos. File hashes and visual features are computed only on your Mac.

移到废纸篓

选择文件后使用工具栏、右键菜单或 Command-Delete。应用会二次确认，不会自动或永久删除文件。

Select files and use the toolbar, context menu, or Command-Delete. The app asks for confirmation and never removes files automatically or permanently.

最近目录与自动刷新

保存最近 10 个目录以便重新授权，并在当前目录变化时自动刷新。可随时清除最近目录。

Keep up to ten recent folders for convenient reauthorization and refresh the current folder automatically when it changes. Recent folders can be cleared at any time.

媒体信息与高级筛选

查看图片尺寸、视频时长等信息，并使用文件大小、日期、分辨率和时长等高级条件筛选排序。

Inspect dimensions, duration, and other media details, then filter and sort by advanced criteria such as size, date, resolution, and duration.

常见问题 · FAQ

为什么看不到某个文件？ · Why is a file missing?

应用只显示 macOS 能识别为图片或视频的普通文件，并跳过隐藏项目、应用包内容和无权访问的目录。确认格式受系统支持后再点击刷新。

The app shows regular files macOS recognizes as images or videos and skips hidden items, app package contents, and inaccessible folders. Confirm the format is supported, then rescan.

为什么缩略图或快速预览失败？ · Why did thumbnail or Quick Look fail?

文件可能已移动、损坏、权限发生变化，或者系统不支持解码该格式。请先在 Finder 中确认文件仍可打开。

The file may have moved, become damaged, lost permission, or use a format the system cannot decode. Confirm that Finder can still open it.

应用会修改我的文件吗？ · Does the app modify my files?

浏览和分析不会修改文件。只有 Pro 用户主动选择文件、执行“移到废纸篓”并二次确认后，文件才会移入 macOS 废纸篓。可以从废纸篓恢复；应用不会永久删除、重命名或编辑文件。

Browsing and analysis do not modify files. A file moves to the macOS Trash only after a Pro user selects it, invokes Move to Trash, and confirms. It remains recoverable; the app does not permanently delete, rename, or edit files.

重复和相似分析会上传文件吗？ · Does duplicate analysis upload files?

不会。图片、视频、哈希、视觉特征和分析结果仅在 Mac 本地处理。分析缓存最多 1 GB，可在应用中清除。

No. Photos, videos, hashes, visual features, and results are processed only on your Mac. The analysis cache is limited to 1 GB and can be cleared in the app.

如何恢复 Pro 购买？ · How do I restore Pro?

打开 Pro 页面并点击“恢复购买”，然后按照 App Store 提示使用原购买 Apple 账号完成。

Open the Pro screen, choose Restore Purchases, and follow the App Store prompts using the Apple Account that owns the purchase.

如何卸载？ · How do I uninstall?

退出应用后，将“本地素材快捷浏览”从“应用程序”文件夹移到废纸篓即可。

Quit the app, then move Local Media Quick Browser from Applications to the Trash.

When reporting an issue, include your macOS version, app version, reproduction steps, and screenshots without private content. Do not upload private media, sensitive paths, or other personal information.
