隐私政策

Privacy Policy

本地素材快捷浏览尊重你的隐私。应用采用本地优先、只读设计，不收集或上传你的个人数据。

Local Media Quick Browser respects your privacy. It is local-first and read-only by design, and does not collect or upload your personal data.

1. 数据收集 · Data Collection

应用不收集、不上传、不出售、不出租或与任何人共享个人数据、使用数据、诊断数据或媒体内容。

The app does not collect, upload, sell, rent, or share personal data, usage data, diagnostic data, or media content.

2. 本地媒体处理 · On-Device Media Processing

图片、视频、缩略图、文件名、相对路径、文件大小、修改日期和视频时长仅在你的 Mac 上处理。应用不会把这些信息传输到开发者或第三方服务器。

Photos, videos, thumbnails, filenames, relative paths, file sizes, modification dates, and video durations are processed only on your Mac. None of this information is sent to the developer or to third-party servers.

3. 文件夹访问 · Folder Access

应用仅访问你通过系统文件夹选择器或拖放主动选择的文件夹，以及其中的后代目录。访问用于显示和预览媒体，应用不会删除、移动、重命名或修改原始文件。

The app accesses only folders you explicitly choose through the system folder picker or drag and drop, including their descendant folders. Access is used to display and preview media. The app does not delete, move, rename, or modify original files.

4. 本机偏好设置 · Local Preferences

应用不会保存你导入过的文件夹路径或媒体索引。排序方式、排序方向和缩略图尺寸等非敏感界面偏好可能通过 macOS 偏好设置保存在本机，直到你重置偏好或移除应用数据。

The app does not save previously imported folder paths or a media index. Non-sensitive interface preferences, such as sort field, sort direction, and thumbnail size, may be stored locally through macOS preferences until you reset them or remove the app's data.

5. 账号、分析与跟踪 · Accounts, Analytics, and Tracking

应用不要求注册账号，不包含广告、分析 SDK、跟踪技术、跨应用跟踪或第三方数据服务。本网站同样不使用 Cookie、分析脚本或联系表单。

The app requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, cross-app tracking, or third-party data services. This website also uses no cookies, analytics scripts, or contact forms.

6. 数据保留与删除 · Data Retention and Deletion

由于开发者不接收用户数据，因此没有服务器端数据需要保留或删除。你可以随时停止使用应用、重置本机偏好，或卸载应用。

Because the developer does not receive user data, there is no server-side user data to retain or delete. You may stop using the app, reset local preferences, or uninstall it at any time.

7. 政策变更 · Changes to This Policy

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本政策将同步更新，并在页面顶部标明新的更新日期。

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and a new revision date will appear at the top of this page.

8. 联系我们 · Contact

如有隐私问题，请发送邮件至 duomiart@gmail.com。

For privacy questions, email duomiart@gmail.com.
